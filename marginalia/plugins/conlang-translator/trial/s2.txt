CHARACTERS
- Garrok: commander of the Imperial legion.
- Torv: a guard of the legion.
- Mara: a human merchant, captured by the legion.
Do not know Vrakht: Mara.

PART
Mara pressed her back against the wall of the tent. Torv leaned over her, his breath sour with wine.

"Where is the gold, merchant?" he snarled in the Imperial tongue.

"I don't understand you!" Mara cried. "Please, I have nothing, I swear it."

Torv laughed and raised his fist, but the flap of the tent opened and Garrok stepped in. He looked at the shaking woman for a long moment.

"You will carry water for my soldiers," he told her in the common tongue, badly, each word like a stone. "Work, and you live."

Then he turned to Torv. "Leave her alone, you fool. She is worth more alive."
