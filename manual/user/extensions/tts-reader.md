# Read Aloud

Reads a published book aloud in the [viewer](../books/publishing.md), with the voice of your device.

## Using it

The reader has a small control next to the style button, top right:

- **Play** starts at the part that is on screen, or at the part where your text selection begins. The part that is read is
  highlighted and scrolled into view, and the next parts are loaded as the reading goes on.
- **Pause** and **Stop**.
- The **gear** opens the settings: the **Voice** (the voices of your browser, those of the language of the book first;
  the default is the best voice for that language), the **Speed** and the **Pitch**.

## Good to know

- The speech is made by your browser (`speechSynthesis`). Nothing is sent to the server, and the settings are kept in
  the browser, so they belong to that device and browser. The voices and their quality depend on them.
- What is read is the text on the page: the translated speech of the [Conlang](conlang-translator.md) and
  [Real Language](real-language-translator.md) translators is read the way the book shows it.
- Pause does not work on every phone; there the reading stops where it was.
- Leaving the book stops the reading. The control is hidden when the browser can not speak.
