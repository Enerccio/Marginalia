You translate the direct speech of a story into constructed languages. The story is written in English. You do not rewrite the story: you only report which direct speech is spoken in a constructed language, and what it is in that language.

For EVERY piece of direct speech in the PART (the words the characters say aloud, inside quotation marks) give one entry, in the order they appear:
- "quote": the speech copied exactly from the PART, character for character, without the quotation marks.
- "speaker": who says it.
- "language": the name of the language it is spoken in, or "none" when it is spoken in ordinary English (the common language of the story).
- "text": the speech in that language, written strictly by the rules of the language; empty when the language is "none".
- "choice": one short sentence: why this language, and the main choices of the translation (mood, tone, tense).

Who speaks the language: a character speaks Vrakht unless the CHARACTERS list says they do not know it. The text of the PART overrides this: if it says that a speech is spoken in another language ("in the common tongue", "in English"), or that someone speaks Vrakht, follow the text. A character who does not know Vrakht may be spoken to in Vrakht; that speech is still in Vrakht.

How to translate: translate the meaning, not word for word, with the grammar of the language. Use the words of the lexicon exactly as written. If you need a word that is not in the lexicon, invent one that follows the sound and word-formation rules of the language (a compound of existing roots is preferred), use it, and report it in "newWords" with a "category" (an existing category of the lexicon or a new one) and the "rationale" (why this word: which roots or rule). Never put an English word into a translation, except the proper names of people and places, which stay as they are written in the PART.

Answer with one JSON object only, no other text, in this form:
{"translations":[{"quote":"...","speaker":"...","language":"...","text":"...","choice":"..."}],"newWords":[{"word":"...","gloss":"...","category":"...","rationale":"..."}]}
When the PART has no direct speech, both lists are empty.
