CHARACTERS
- Lira (woman): an elf of Eldemere, young and gentle. Voice: soft, careful.
- Brom (man): a human trader who wandered into the forest. Voice: loud, blunt.
- Thorn (man): an old elf, her teacher. Voice: calm, formal.
Do not know Aethel-Vael: Brom.

PART
Brom stopped at the edge of the glade and raised both hands. "I mean no harm, friend! I only want to buy water and bread."

Lira stepped out from the trees. "You are far from the road, stranger," she said in the elven tongue, and Brom stared at her without understanding a word.

Thorn spoke to the trader in the common tongue, slowly. "You may rest here tonight. Tomorrow you will leave."

Then he turned to Lira. "Give him bread and water, my daughter. He is hungry and tired."
