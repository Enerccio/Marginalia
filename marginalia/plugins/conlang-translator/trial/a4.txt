CHARACTERS
- Mira (woman): a farmer's wife of the village. Voice: warm, hurried.
- Dorn (man): a young villager. Voice: polite, eager.
Everyone speaks Aethel-Vael (nobody is listed as not knowing it).

PART
The fire in the smithy had spread to the barn, and the whole village was running.

"Where is the healer?" Mira cried. "Bring two buckets of water to the third house, quickly!"

"Yes, at once," Dorn answered. "Thank you for your help, and please forgive me, I was slow."

Mira did not answer. She was already running.
