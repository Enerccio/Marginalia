CHARACTERS
- Selene (woman): the healer of the village. Voice: formal, precise, speaks like a physician.
- Varek (man): a drunk mercenary. Voice: crude, slangy, loud.
Everyone speaks Aethel-Vael (nobody is listed as not knowing it).

PART
The wounded girl lay on the table, pale and wet with fever, the arrow still in her side. Selene bent over her and cut away the cloth.

"The arrow struck below the breast," she said to the girl's mother. "She has a fever, and her skin is cold and wet."

Varek leaned in the doorway with a jug in his hand and grinned at the girl. "Fine face on that one, boys. Finer tits."

Selene did not look up. "Leave this house at once."
