CHARACTERS
- Garrok: commander of the Imperial legion, hard and proud.
- Brann: a soldier of the legion, his subordinate.
Everyone speaks Vrakht (nobody is listed as not knowing it).

PART
Garrok studied the burning village from the ridge, his arms crossed over the iron of his armor. "Report," he said without turning to Brann. "How many fled?"

Brann saluted. "Three dozen, my lord. Most of them went north through the pass."

Garrok's jaw tightened. "Then we do not wait for the morning. Bring the legion to the pass before the sun is up, or I will have your head on a pike!"

"At once, my lord," Brann said, and ran.
