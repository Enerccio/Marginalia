# Conlang Translator

Stub, nothing works yet. The plan is in [TODO.md](TODO.md).

The idea: you describe a constructed language (its sounds, grammar, vocabulary) and the plugin has the model translate the
direct speech of the characters who speak it into the language, once a part is final. The reader clicks the translated speech
to see the original, in the viewer and in the manuscript dialog; the exports can print the original as footnotes.
