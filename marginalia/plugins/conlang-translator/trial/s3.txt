CHARACTERS
- Ilsa: the healer of the legion, tired and short-tempered.
- Brann: a soldier of the legion.
Everyone speaks Vrakht (nobody is listed as not knowing it).

PART
The wounded lay in rows in the third tent, and the healer had not slept in two days. Brann ducked in under the flap with a bucket in each hand.

"Bring the water here, and clean cloth, quickly," Ilsa said. "The fever is spreading and the bandages are gone."

"The quartermaster says there is no more cloth," Brann answered. "Only the tent canvas."

"Then cut the canvas!"
