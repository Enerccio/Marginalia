CHARACTERS
- Garrok: commander of the Imperial legion.
- Brann: a soldier of the legion.
Everyone speaks Vrakht (nobody is listed as not knowing it).

PART
Rain fell on the camp for three days. Garrok told Brann to double the guard on the north road, and Brann went out into the mud without a word. By the end of the week the river had risen over the ford.
