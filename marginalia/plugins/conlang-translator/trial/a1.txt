CHARACTERS
- Lira (woman): an elf of Eldemere, young and gentle. Voice: soft, careful.
- Thorn (man): an old elf, her teacher. Voice: calm, formal.
Everyone speaks Aethel-Vael (nobody is listed as not knowing it).

PART
Lira knelt by the river as dusk gathered over Eldemere. "I do not see the stars because the night is dark," she said softly.

Thorn laid his hand on her shoulder. "The light of the moon will protect the forest," he answered. "The stars always return."

"I fear the dark mountains," Lira whispered. "They will destroy the old trees."

"We protected the forest yesterday," Thorn said, "and we will protect it tomorrow."
